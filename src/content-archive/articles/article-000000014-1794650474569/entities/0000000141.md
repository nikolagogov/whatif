Imagine that the Library of Alexandria never burned. Instead of disappearing through centuries of conflict, neglect, and political change, it survived as a protected institution. Its scholars continued to collect manuscripts, debate ideas, and preserve discoveries from across the known world.

What would happen if humanity had never lost one of its greatest centres of knowledge?

The answer might change not only the history of science, but also the relationship between civilizations.
