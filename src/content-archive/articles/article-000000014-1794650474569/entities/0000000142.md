## The Decision That Changed History

In the third century BC, the Ptolemaic rulers make an unexpected decision: the Library of Alexandria receives permanent legal protection and independent funding.

A council of scholars is established to oversee its collections. No ruler can destroy or remove its manuscripts without the approval of the council.

### A New Institution

The Library gradually becomes more than a collection of scrolls. It develops workshops for copying manuscripts, schools for mathematics and astronomy, and a permanent community of translators.

Its doors remain open to scholars from Greece, Egypt, Persia, India, and beyond.

### The First Great Archive

By the first century BC, the institution has developed an extensive catalogue. Every manuscript is copied several times, and important works are distributed to smaller libraries throughout the Mediterranean.

Knowledge becomes harder to lose because it no longer depends on a single building.
