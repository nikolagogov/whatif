## Rome Inherits a Living Tradition

When Rome expands into Egypt, the Library becomes an important political and intellectual institution.

Roman officials initially attempt to control its administration. However, the value of its scholars and technical knowledge soon becomes apparent.

### Science and Engineering

Roman engineers gain access to centuries of mathematical and mechanical research. New generations of scholars combine Greek geometry with practical Roman engineering.

Possible developments include:

- More advanced surveying methods.
- Improved mechanical devices.
- Better astronomical observations.
- A wider network of technical schools.

### The Mediterranean Network

Alexandria establishes partnerships with institutions in Athens, Rome, Antioch, and Carthage.

The Mediterranean gradually becomes a connected intellectual space.
