## A World That Never Forgot

In this alternate timeline, the Library of Alexandria becomes a symbol of continuity.

It does not prevent wars, political collapse, or periods of intellectual stagnation. But it makes one thing less likely: the permanent disappearance of entire bodies of human knowledge.

Perhaps the greatest difference is not that humanity becomes more intelligent, but that every generation begins with more of what the previous one learned.

> Civilization advances not only by discovering new things, but also by refusing to forget what it has already discovered.
